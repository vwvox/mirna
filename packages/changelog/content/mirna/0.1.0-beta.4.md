- Recordings that mix languages, like a Chinese lesson taught in English, now go to Qwen3-ASR instead of Parakeet.
- When Parakeet wrote a transcript but wasn't sure of the language, the line above it offers Transcribe again with Qwen3-ASR.
- Settings › General › Help › Export logs saves one file, in Downloads, with what Mirna did and any errors on this Mac and your paired phones. Send it to whoever is helping you. It doesn't include your notes, transcripts or recordings.

## On your phone

Mirna for Android 0.1.11.

- Transcribe again on Mac, from a note's ⋯ menu: Automatic, Qwen3-ASR or Parakeet, and the summary can be written again too.
- Trim a recording that ran on, from the same menu: cut the last 5 to 60 minutes, or drag to where it should end. Mirna suggests where the talking stopped and lets you hear the 5 seconds before the cut.
- Forgotten recordings look after themselves: "Still recording?" after 15 minutes of quiet, an automatic pause after 30, a check-in at 2 hours, and a new part every 3 hours.
- Show pinyin over Chinese in transcripts.
- Saving a long recording is quicker, and no longer stalls with the screen off.
- Settings › About › Export log, to share what happened on the phone.
- Syncs with your Mac over the phone's own hotspot.
