- Pair an Android phone in Settings › Phone by scanning the code with Mirna on the phone.
- Recordings made on the phone come to this Mac over your own Wi-Fi. The Mac transcribes and summarizes them, then sends the text back. Audio never goes back to the phone.
- Titles, summaries, transcripts and notes stay in sync both ways.

## On your phone

Mirna for Android 0.1.10.

- Records with the screen off, with the timer on the lock screen and Pause and Stop in the notification.
- Sends recordings on its own on Wi-Fi, or only when you tap Sync now.
- Can also show notes recorded on the Mac, as text.
