summary: "The first beta of Mirna: meeting notes written on your Mac, with nothing leaving it unless you choose a cloud service."
---

- Mirna records meetings and voice notes, transcribes them on your Mac and writes a summary with a model you choose. There's no account, no analytics and no update checks.
- Transcribes with Parakeet by default, which knows 25 European languages, and switches to Qwen3-ASR when it hears another language, such as Chinese.
- Takes out the echo when your microphone picks up your speakers. Headphones still give the cleanest recording.
- Shows pinyin over Chinese in transcripts. Turn it on with Show pinyin above the transcript.
- Transcribe a note again with another engine from the … menu. The speakers you named stay, and Mirna offers to update the summary.
- Marks you as a speaker, so the AI knows who's asking.
- Names a note from its summary's heading.
- Home (⌘⇧H) brings you back to the start.
- Local models start with thinking off, and warm up as a recording stops, so summaries start sooner.
