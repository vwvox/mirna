- Lessons: start one, or make a note one from its ⋯ menu, and file it in a course. Mark moments during class with ⌥1, ⌥2 and ⌥3, or from the floating bar.
- After class, Mirna writes a study sheet: new words with the teacher's own clips, patterns, corrections, homework and how much each of you talked.
- Lessons work for any language, for topics like history or cooking (key terms, ideas and sources), and for maths and science (formulas, worked examples and problems).
- Practise with spaced repetition: listen and say, read, or shadow a sentence, with a pitch chart for Mandarin tones. Words export to Anki and Pleco.
- Each course has its own page: words due, homework, progress over the lessons and every word of the course.
- Review a lesson in 5 or 10 minutes, full window, from the Review button on its study sheet. It replays the teacher's clips, and what you miss comes back in Practise.
- The teacher's clips play in both ears.
- Pause a recording from its note, the floating bar or the menu bar. A forgotten recording is looked after as on the phone: "Still recording?" after 15 minutes of quiet, and a pause after 30.
- Trim a recording that ran on, from a note's ⋯ menu.
- Settings › Dictation can use its own speech and cleanup models, soft start and finish sounds, and a writing style for each kind of app. A cancel button sits next to Done.
- Before your first recording, Mirna asks you to check the rules where you and the people you record are, and to tell them first.
- Mirna asks before remembering someone's voice, and Remember speakers is now off by default.
- A live recording that isn't in English is transcribed again once it ends, instead of keeping a garbled transcript.
- Long live recordings no longer repeat words.

## On your phone

Mirna for Android 0.1.12.

- Lessons come to your phone with their study sheet and the teacher's clips, even with notes recorded on the Mac switched off.
- Review a lesson on the go: the same 5- or 10-minute review as on the Mac, sized for the phone, and it works offline. Your Mac counts it at the next sync.
- Say a recording is a lesson while you record it, or later from its ⋯ menu. The Mac writes the sheet and the review and sends them back.
- A recording keeps going when you leave its note, and Record more adds to a note you recorded on the phone.
